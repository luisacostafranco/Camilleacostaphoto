Frequently Asked Questions

WHAT SHOULD I WEAR?⌄

Think coordinate, don't match! Choose a neutral color scheme and include different layers; for example, someone in denim, someone in cotton, someone in a dress, someone in shorts. Avoid loud patterns — featuring one person in a soft pattern is great! The last thing you want are matching white t-shirts.

HOW MANY PHOTOS WILL I RECEIVE?⌄

About 50 photos, sometimes more!

HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE TO GET OUR PHOTOS?⌄

I should have them back to you within 2 weeks!

CAN YOU RECOMMEND ANY GOOD LOCATIONS?⌄

Absolutely. I have plenty of suggestions for the Tooele County area and can help you choose a location that fits the look you want.
