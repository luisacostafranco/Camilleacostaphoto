FAQ

Before your session.

What should I wear?+

Think coordinated, not matching. Neutral colors, soft texture, and layers photograph beautifully. Avoid large logos and very bright patterns.

How many photos will I receive?+

Gallery size varies, but every gallery is curated to include the strongest portraits, candids, and details.

How long until I get my photos?+

Typical turnaround is about two to three weeks.

Can you recommend locations?+

Absolutely. I can recommend Tooele-area locations based on season, scenery, accessibility, and the look you want.
