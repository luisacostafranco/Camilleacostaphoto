Frequently Asked Questions

WHAT SHOULD I WEAR?

Think coordinate, don't match! Chose a neutral color scheme and include different layers; for example, someone in denim, someone in cotton, someone in a dress, someone in shorts. Avoid loud patterns-featuring one person in a soft pattern is great! The last thing you want are matching white t-shirts.

HOW MANY PHOTOS WILL I RECEIVE?

For a full session, expect a curated gallery of professionally edited images. The exact number varies based on the session and how much variety we capture.

HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE TO GET OUR PHOTOS?

Typical turnaround is approximately two to three weeks. If anything changes, you'll receive an update.

CAN YOU RECOMMEND ANY GOOD LOCATIONS?

Absolutely. I can recommend Tooele-area locations based on your preferred look, season, lighting, and how far you'd like to travel.
